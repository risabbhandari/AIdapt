# AIdapt
AIdapt

Helping the workforce work alongside AI.

AIdapt is a platform that helps professionals and organizations integrate AI into real workflows through guided AI processes. The goal is to increase productivity while ensuring AI augments human work rather than replaces it.

This repository contains the initial landing page and waitlist website for the AIdapt platform.

Vision

AI is rapidly transforming the way work gets done.

However, most professionals and companies struggle to integrate AI into their everyday workflows in a practical way.

AIdapt exists to help the workforce adapt to the AI economy, enabling people to work alongside AI rather than be displaced by it.

What AIdapt Will Do

The long-term product vision includes:

• Guided AI workflows for real work tasks
• AI productivity tools embedded in everyday operations
• Workflow automation with human-in-the-loop control
• AI adoption tools for organizations
• Professional AI skill adaptation tools
